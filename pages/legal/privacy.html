Privacy Policy

Draft — pending review and approval

This draft describes the intended website workflow and retention periods. Email, SharePoint and retention settings must be verified before this notice is published as final.

AMNIKON Technologies uses information submitted through this website to respond to enquiries, discuss service requirements and consider expressions of interest for future opportunities.

Information you provide

Depending on the form, this may include your name, email address, company, enquiry or service-request details, resume and information about your professional experience.

How we use it

We use this information to handle your request, communicate with you, assess applications for suitable opportunities and help protect the website against spam and abuse.

Resumes for future opportunities

Resume submissions are general expressions of interest; there are no current vacancies. In the intended workflow, resumes are forwarded to our HR team and stored in our designated Microsoft SharePoint library. HR may contact you if a suitable opportunity becomes available and you are shortlisted.

Service providers

Our website hosting, email and Microsoft 365 services may process information as part of delivering these functions.

When enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile helps verify form submissions to prevent spam and abuse. Related data-processing details will be confirmed before this policy is published.

How long we keep your information

We normally keep resumes and related candidate information for up to six months from submission so we can consider you for future opportunities. This applies to our HR email and designated SharePoint copies.

We normally keep website enquiry messages for up to 12 months from receipt to respond to questions and maintain a record of the correspondence. If an enquiry leads to a customer engagement, the necessary business records are managed under the retention arrangements for that engagement.

Information may be kept longer where needed to meet a legal obligation, resolve a dispute or maintain a required business record. Access to any retained information remains restricted. Deleted information may remain temporarily in protected recovery systems or backups until their retention cycle ends.

You can ask us to delete or update information you have submitted by contacting the email address below. We will review the request and explain any reason we need to retain particular records.

Website storage and external resources

The website uses session storage to help avoid duplicate form submissions. This stores a submission reference and a fingerprint of the submission rather than the entered form details or resume file.

Some fonts and website libraries load from external providers. Those providers may receive technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests these resources. Hosting and security services may also process technical request information to operate and protect the website.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have submitted, email info@amnikontechnologies.com.
