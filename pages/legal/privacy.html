Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

AMNIKON Technologies uses information submitted through this website to respond to enquiries and support requests, discuss services, consider resumes for future opportunities, and protect the website from spam or misuse.

Information collected

Depending on the form, information may include your name, email address, company, selected service, message or support details, resume, and professional experience. Hosting and security systems may also process technical request information such as browser and connection details.

Resume submissions

There are no current vacancies. A resume is a general expression of interest, not an application for an advertised role. Resume submissions are shared with AMNIKON's HR team, delivered by email, and stored in the AMNIKON RESUME_WEBSITE SharePoint library in its Resumes folder. HR may contact you if a suitable role becomes available and you are shortlisted. Submission does not guarantee an interview or employment.

Service providers and website technology

Staff who need the information to handle your request may access it. Website hosting, email delivery, and Microsoft 365 providers process information to provide those services. External fonts, libraries, or maps may also receive technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests them. AMNIKON does not sell website form submissions or use them for advertising.

The website uses session storage to prevent duplicate form submissions. It stores a submission reference and fingerprint, not the form contents or uploaded resume.

Retention and requests

Information is retained only as long as needed to handle the request, consider a resume for future opportunities, maintain necessary business records, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Service-provider backups may persist for a limited period under their backup cycles. You may ask AMNIKON to provide, correct, or delete information you submitted by emailing info@amnikontechnologies.com. Requests are reviewed against applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have submitted, email info@amnikontechnologies.com.
