Frequently Asked Questions

Cybersecurity Questions, Answered.

Find answers to common questions about our cybersecurity services, security monitoring, protection, compliance, and incident response capabilities.

What cybersecurity services do you provide?
We provide endpoint security, threat monitoring, SOC services, incident response, vulnerability management, Zero Trust, and security compliance support.
Do you provide 24×7 security monitoring?
Yes. We provide continuous security monitoring to help identify and respond to potential threats around the clock.
Can you protect our endpoints and devices?
Yes. We help secure laptops, desktops, servers, and other endpoints against malware, unauthorized access, and emerging threats.
Do you provide Security Operations Center (SOC) services?
Yes. Our SOC services provide centralized monitoring, threat analysis, alert management, and security incident support.
Can you help implement a Zero Trust security strategy?
Yes. We can help organizations implement Zero Trust principles around identity, access, devices, applications, and network security.
Do you provide vulnerability assessment and management?
Yes. We identify security weaknesses and help prioritize remediation based on risk and business impact.
Do you provide incident response and remediation?
Yes. We assist with investigating security incidents, containing threats, restoring affected systems, and strengthening defenses afterward.
Can you provide ongoing cybersecurity support?
Yes. We provide ongoing security monitoring, maintenance, threat analysis, and technical support to help maintain your security posture.
