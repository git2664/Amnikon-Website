Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions, answered

How is a professional services engagement scoped?

We agree objectives, deliverables, responsibilities, assumptions and acceptance criteria before delivery starts.

Can you work alongside our IT team and vendors?

Yes. Responsibilities and communication routes can be agreed so implementation fits your existing team and supplier arrangements.

How do you manage changes to live systems?

Change plans should include dependencies, approvals, maintenance windows, validation and rollback steps appropriate to the environment.

Is ongoing support included after the project?

Handover and any post-project support period are defined in the scope. Ongoing managed services can be discussed separately.
