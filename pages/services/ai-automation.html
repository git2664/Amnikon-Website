AI & Automation FAQs

Choosing a useful starting point

Does every automated workflow need AI?

No. Predictable tasks often work well with defined rules. AI is considered when the task involves less structured information, with appropriate checks on its outputs.

What makes a useful first pilot?

A repeatable task with a clear owner, available inputs and a measurable result. A contained pilot helps test value and exceptions before wider rollout.

Where do people stay involved?

People can review extracted information, approve changes and handle exceptions. Review points are designed around the effect an error could have.

Can an assistant use confidential company information?

Data sources, access permissions, service providers and usage boundaries must be agreed before connecting confidential information. The assistant should respect the access rules of the approved environment.
