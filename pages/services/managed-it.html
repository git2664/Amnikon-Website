Everything You Need To Know

Answers to some of the most common questions about our Managed IT Services.

Do you provide 24/7 IT support?
Yes. Our team continuously monitors your infrastructure and provides around-the-clock technical support to minimize downtime.
Do you provide remote IT support for end users?
Yes. Our support team provides remote assistance for desktops, laptops, applications, user accounts and common IT issues. Onsite support can also be provided when required.
Do you manage servers and IT infrastructure?
Yes. We manage and maintain Windows and Linux servers, virtualization platforms, storage systems and other core IT infrastructure.
Do you manage network infrastructure?
Yes. We manage network infrastructure including switches, routers, firewalls, Wi-Fi, VPN connectivity and network performance.
Can you manage IT assets and employee devices?
Yes. We can maintain hardware and software inventories, manage device lifecycles and support employee computers and other IT assets.
Do you provide IT service desk and ticket management?
Yes. Users can raise IT incidents and service requests through a centralized service desk, allowing issues to be tracked, prioritized and resolved efficiently.
Can you provide IT infrastructure assessments?
Yes. We assess your existing infrastructure, identify improvement opportunities and provide practical recommendations for performance, reliability and scalability.
Do you provide backup and recovery management?
Yes. We manage backup operations, monitor backup health and support data recovery and disaster recovery requirements.
